How this works
  1. Case files. The text of this site (projects, skills, experience, education, awards) is split into short passages.
  2. Embeddings. Each passage is turned into numbers that capture its meaning (Google Gemini embeddings) and stored in a vector database.
  3. Hybrid search. Your question is matched by meaning and by keywords, and the best passages are picked.
  4. Grounded answer. A language model (Groq) answers only from those passages and cites them; if they do not cover the question, it says so instead of guessing.

Nothing you type is used to train a model. Questions are logged without your IP address (a salted hash only) to check answer quality. Do not share personal details.

      Privacy

      This site counts anonymous daily totals (for example, how many times the chat was opened). Those counts hold no cookies, no IP address and no identity, there is no third-party analytics, and nothing is sent if your browser says Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

      If you send a telegram, your name, email and message are stored so I can reply, together with a salted hash of your IP address for spam limits. Chat questions are stored without your IP address (a salted hash only) to check answer quality. Voice recordings are never stored. I aim to delete telegrams after about a year and chat questions after about 90 days.

      Questions or a deletion request: use the contact form or the email address in the Telegraph section.
