**Decision:** `RAG_MIN_SIMILARITY` stays at **0.55**. Similarity alone cannot refuse the unanswerable questions without also refusing real ones (every question is about the same person, so scores cluster at 0.5 to 0.8), and wrongly refusing a real question is worse than spending one LLM call. The gate is a cheap pre-filter for plainly off-topic questions; the 5/5 refusals above come from the grounded prompt's "not in my case files" rule (r01 to r04) and the private-data screen (r05), verified end to end.

**Chosen models:** `LLM_MODEL = qwen/qwen3.8-27b` (15/15, 5/5, p95 2.1 s: tied on accuracy with gpt-oss-120b and the fastest), `LLM_SECONDARY_MODEL = openai/gpt-oss-120b`, `LLM_FALLBACK_MODEL = gemini-3.5-flash`. gpt-oss-20b was rejected: it garbled "NED" in the Urdu answer (14/15). The Gemini fallback was not exercised by the evaluation (0/20 fallbacks); it is covered by mocked tests and was flaky (503 / time-outs) in live probes, which is why a second Groq model sits behind it.

**Two defects the first evaluation exposed (both fixed, all models re-run):** an answer that ended with a caveat ("that is not in my case files regarding other specifics") was wrongly flagged as a refusal and lost its sources (a refusal now has to be the start of the reply), and the fact matcher ignored typographic variants such as no-break spaces and Arabic vowel marks.
