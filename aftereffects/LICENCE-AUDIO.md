# Audio licence: "The Case File of Umer" promo film

All music and sound effects in the promo film are **original works by Muhammad Umer Farooq**. They are generated by code in this repository. **No third-party samples**, loops, stock audio or sound libraries are used, so they are properly licensed for this film, the portfolio and the Module 3 submission.

## What was generated, and by which files

| Output (git-ignored, regenerated on demand) | Content |
|---|---|
| `aftereffects/audio/music.wav` | The score: a spaghetti-western cue in A minor at 96 bpm, 18 bars (45.000 s). The melody was written for this film and quotes no film theme. |
| `aftereffects/audio/sfx.wav` | One synthesised sound per cue in the cue sheet (stamp, typewriter, anvil, pin, ratchet, woodblock, train whistle and chuff, hooves, badge chime, fireworks, spur jingle, projector). |
| `aftereffects/audio/ambience.wav` | Sea wash, wind and night crickets. |
| `aftereffects/audio/mix.wav` | The reference mix of the three stems. |

The generating files:

- `scripts/promo/audio/score.mjs`: the composition, as note data and timing.
- `scripts/promo/audio/render.js`: the offline synthesis, using `OfflineAudioContext` at 48 kHz stereo.
- `scripts/promo/audio/render.html`: the page that runs `render.js` on the dev server.
- `scripts/promo/render-audio.mjs`: runs the render. It also measures loudness with ffmpeg, applies gain and the peak limiter, and writes the 24-bit WAVs.
- `src/explore/music.js`: the site's own score voices, reused here:
  - Karplus-Strong guitar
  - spring reverb
  - whistle and harmonica voices
  - kick, tambourine, shaker, anvil and bell
- `src/explore/music-core.js`: pitch helpers.
- `docs/promo/cues.json`: the cue sheet. Every sound effect starts on its frame's exact sample (frame × 2000 at 48 kHz). The file is generated by `scripts/promo/build-cues.mjs`.

**Regenerate:** `node scripts/promo/render-audio.mjs`. The render is deterministic, because every noise source is seeded.

**Tools:**
- the browser's Web Audio API, in Google Chrome;
- ffmpeg, for loudness analysis only. ffmpeg contributes no audio content.
