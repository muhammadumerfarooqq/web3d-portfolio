I'm Muhammad Umer Farooq, an Associate AI Engineer at Axisero and a Computer Science & Information Technology student at NED University of Engineering and Technology in Karachi.

I love the moment an idea stops being a demo and starts carrying real weight. At Axisero, the RAG knowledge-base pipeline I built for KnoDesk now gives 10+ businesses document-grounded answers instead of generic chatbot replies, and the Sales Pipeline feature I shipped is opened every day by 25+ colleagues.

At university, I owned the architecture of TALEEM360 — an AI-powered, bilingual 3D learning app that turns flat textbook diagrams into interactive 3D models, with an AI tutor called Chirag that explains them in English and Urdu. It won the Best Final Year Design Project award at the FYDP Exhibition, TechFest '26, ranking first among 70+ teams.

My toolkit lives on two sides of the same screen: LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation and computer vision on one side; Three.js, Blender, After Effects, Unity and Unreal Engine on the other. That's why this portfolio is a 3D world with an AI you can talk to — the avatar was built in Blender, and the chatbot answers only from my own documents.

I care about evidence over hype: evaluation harnesses, real test scenarios, and fixing the production bugs nobody asked me to find.
