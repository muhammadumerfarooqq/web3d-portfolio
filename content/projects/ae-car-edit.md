## About
APEX is a 25-second cinematic car commercial I made in Adobe After Effects as a two-person university project, cut to music from gimbal footage and stock clips.

## Techniques
- Audio sync with beat markers, and speed ramping with time remapping and graph-editor easing.
- Motion-tracked stabilization, motion blur and camera shake for a handheld, high-speed feel.
- A camera-viewfinder HUD: corner frame marks, a running timecode, scene labels like "URBAN CIRCUIT — NIGHT", and animated spec callouts ("0–100 KM/H 3.2S", "TOP SPEED 290 KM/H").
- Kinetic title typography and an end card: "Feel the Drive. #DriveApex".
- ExtendScript to automate the repetitive parts of the workflow.

The version on this site is muted.
