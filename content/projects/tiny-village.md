## About
Tiny Village is a survival game built in Unreal Engine: the survivor must restore four generators while avoiding red enemies. A story intro cinematic plays before the title screen (with a skip option) and leads into the gameplay. It was built to be showcased at a game development competition with 20 competing teams.
