## The problem
Students in Sindh study from textbooks full of static diagrams — a heart, a motor, an atom — that are hard to imagine in three dimensions, and many study in Urdu-medium schools.

## What I built
TALEEM360 turns static Sindh Textbook Board diagrams into interactive 3D models. Students scan a textbook diagram, and the app fetches the matching 3D model so they can rotate it, zoom in and explore it part by part. The project is scoped to 500+ diagrams across Classes 6–10 and four STEM subjects (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Computer Science).

It includes **Chirag**, a RAG-based AI tutor that answers students' questions grounded in textbook content, with bilingual English/Urdu text and voice-over for Urdu-medium learners. There's a student module and a teacher module, including a classroom projector mode.

## My role
I owned the architecture through two pivots. When ARCore didn't run on the budget Android phones students actually use, we moved to a scan-then-view approach that works on low-end devices.

## Result
TALEEM360 won the **Best Final Year Design Project award at the FYDP Exhibition, TechFest '26 (24 October 2026)**, ranking first among 70+ teams. It's live at taleem360.vercel.app.
